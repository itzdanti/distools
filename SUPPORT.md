# Support

Need a hand, found a bug, or want to suggest a tool? Here is where to go.

## Read the docs first

Most questions are answered there:

- [Getting started](https://distools.itzdanti.dev/docs/getting-started) — the tools and which ones
  use the network
- [API](https://distools.itzdanti.dev/docs/api) — every endpoint, with examples
- [Privacy](https://distools.itzdanti.dev/docs/privacy) — what is stored and what is sent

## Something is broken

Open an issue with the **Bug report** template:

<https://github.com/itzdanti/distools/issues/new/choose>

Include:

- the tool or API endpoint,
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened,
- your browser and operating system,
- and the exact error text or a screenshot, if there is one.

If the tool is one that makes a network request, mention whether it returned `401`. Lookups for
servers and profiles now commonly do, and that is Discord, not the tool.

## A tool idea

Open an issue with the **Tool request** template. Say what the tool would do, who it is for, and
whether it needs the network. Tool ideas that map to real, harmless, everyday needs are the most
likely to be built.

## Security

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and email
instead.

## Legal and takedowns

Copyright or legal complaints go to **legal@itzdanti.dev**. See the
[Terms of Service](https://distools.itzdanti.dev/terms).

## Contact

Anything else: **distools@itzdanti.dev**.

## What not to ask

- For a bot token, or help obtaining one.
- To add tools for logging messages, validating Nitro gift codes, or generating credentials that
  look real. Those exist to defraud people and will be declined.
- For support using a webhook you do not own.

Please also read the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before posting. Be kind; this is a free
project maintained in someone's spare time.
